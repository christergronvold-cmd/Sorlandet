now live grid
Positions from three sources at once: the ship's own position page at fullriggeren.no, the Norwegian Coastal Administration via BarentsWatch, and aisstream.io · weather and sea state from Open-Meteo · map by OpenStreetMap · satellite imagery from NASA GIBS/Worldview · voyage plan from the A+ World Academy 2026–2027 plan, which is tentative and subject to change.
The wind arrows and wave chevrons are a forecast, not measurements from on board. Both point the way they are travelling. The grid follows the map, so it covers whatever is on screen; when it cannot be fetched it falls back to a 360 x 360 nautical mile box around the ship. The slider steps one hour at a time, from two days back to three days ahead.
AIS is picked up by shore-based receivers. Far from land there can be days between positions, and the page then shows the last known one. This is a private family project, not an official source from the ship or the school.
