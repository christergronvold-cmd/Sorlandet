Positions from three sources at once: the ship's own position page at fullriggeren.no, the Norwegian Coastal Administration via BarentsWatch, and aisstream.io · weather and sea state from Open-Meteo · map by OpenStreetMap · voyage plan from the A+ World Academy 2026–2027 plan, which is tentative and subject to change.
The wind arrows and wave chevrons are a forecast for a 360 x 360 nautical mile box around the ship, not measurements from on board. Both point the way they are travelling, and the slider steps forward in 3-hour intervals.
AIS is picked up by shore-based receivers. Far from land there can be days between positions, and the page then shows the last known one. This is a private family project, not an official source from the ship or the school.
